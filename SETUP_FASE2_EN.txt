WHAT IT DOES
  The moment anyone taps MANDAR / APROBAR Y MANDAR / RECHAZAR or the
  office generates a pickup TICKET in the orders app, the order is
  registered in the database - BEFORE Messages opens. If the foreman
  never sends the text, you still see it in the command center.
  app.js is untouched. A new file, pedidos_db.js, does the work.

STEP 1 · RUN THE SQL
  Supabase -> SQL Editor -> New query -> paste ALL of supabase_fase2.sql
  -> RUN. Under Messages/Notices you should see PRUEBA 1/6 .. 6/6 OK and
  PRUEBAS LIMPIADAS. The Results panel ends with:
      OK · Fase 2 lista | pedidos_registrados 0 | siguiente_folio S1001
  Safe to run again at any time.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THE MANDO (PEDIDOS view)
  POR ATENDER          oldest first. "supervisor · 3 h" = waiting on a
                       supervisor. "PO · 4 h" = approved, waiting on YOU.
  ESPERAN SUPERVISOR   turns red when the oldest is 2 h+.
  APROBADOS SIN PO     your queue.
  TIEMPO A APROBAR     median minutes from MANDAR to APROBAR.
  ESTIMADO             sum at catalog price (approved lines if approved,
                       requested lines if not). Not invoice money yet.
  BUSCARON Y NO ENCONTRARON   words foremen typed that found nothing.
                       This is Claudia's catalog to-do list.
  LO MÁS PEDIDO        live lines in the range, by number of orders.
  Detail (click a row) LO QUE PIDIÓ vs LO QUE APROBÓ EL SUPERVISOR vs
                       TICKET, with "(pidió 2)" on trimmed lines and
                       QUITADO / NO AUTORIZADO struck through.
  ⬇ CSV                exports the table on screen, one row per order,
                       lines in the last column.

FLAGS
  RED    Sin respuesta del supervisor 2 h+ · Aprobado sin PO 4 h+ ·
         Abrió Mensajes y no confirmó · Mayordomo se aprobó a sí mismo
  AMBER  Obra escrita a mano · Sin obra · Obra distinta al rol de la
         semana · N fuera de catálogo · N quitada(s) por supervisor ·
         Agregado al pedido anterior · Domingo · Fuera de horario
  Thresholds live in the view material_orders_full. Tell me and I move them.

FOLIO vs PO
  S1001, S1002... is the internal request number the database assigns.
  It is NOT the PO. The PO is whatever you type in the pickup ticket;
  it lands in the "po" column and the row becomes PO ASIGNADO.
  Practice-mode orders get a folio too but are flagged is_practice and
  excluded from every count.

TABLES (Table Editor)
  material_orders        one row per order, its whole life
  material_order_lines   one snapshot per stage: SOLICITADO / APROBADO / TICKET
  material_order_events  who tapped what, when
  material_orders_full   (view) what the mando reads, with flags
