Open it on a LAPTOP. Log in with the same email/password you created
in Supabase -> Authentication -> Users. Only emails listed in the
office_users table can get in. Foremen cannot see this page.

It refreshes itself every 7 seconds. Nobody has to send you anything.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
TOP ROW  (switch the range with HOY / 7 DÍAS / 30 DÍAS / TODO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOY                POs today, split diesel vs gasoline
  7 DÍAS             count for the range + average per day
  TIEMPO POR PO      median seconds a person spends in the app
  ALERTAS ROJAS      hard problems, with amber count underneath
  EN EL APP AHORA    who is using it this minute
  PERSONAS           how many distinct people fueled
  VEHÍCULOS          how many units of the fleet were used

--------------------------------------------------------------------
PANELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  ACTIVIDAD EN VIVO   Who is in the app right now and which step they
                      are on ("paso 4"), or "✓ generó PO". Last 6 min.
  POs POR DÍA         Volume per day. Hover a bar for the number.
  COMBUSTIBLE         Diesel vs gasoline share.
  ESTACIÓN            Tex-Con vs Leo's share. This is your negotiating
                      number: today it is roughly 75/25 to Tex-Con.
  TIPO DE UNIDAD      Trucks vs machinery vs drums vs fuel trucks.
  CARGAS POR OBRA     Which jobsites burn the most fuel. Top 10.
  DÓNDE SE TARDAN     Median seconds per step of the wizard, measured
                      from real use. If one step is slow, that is the
                      step to simplify. Nothing to guess.
  ALERTAS ROJAS       The list to actually work through.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
TABS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  POs        Every PO: number, time, person, unit, fuel, reading,
             jobsite, station, TIME IN THE APP, and flags.
             Click any row for the full record.
  FLOTA      Every vehicle: assigned to, fuel, plate (or "dictada" =
             typed by the person = go enter it in Supabase, or "falta"),
             total fills, fills in 7 days, last reading, and Δ SINCE
             LAST FILL. A negative Δ or a tiny Δ is where fuel theft
             or a wrong odometer shows up.
  PERSONAS   Per person: fills, how many different vehicles they
             fueled, median time per PO, Tex-Con vs Leo's split, and
             total flags. Someone fueling 3+ vehicles is worth a look.

  Search box filters all of it by PO, name, plate or jobsite.
  ⬇ CSV exports exactly what is on screen, flags included.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
FLAGS AND WHAT THEY MEAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  RED
    2ª carga en N h        same vehicle fueled twice inside 24 hours
    Lectura retrocedió     odometer/hours went backwards
  AMBER
    Placa dictada          plate typed by the person, not registered
    N mi desde la última   under 40 miles since the last fill
    Obra ≠ rol             fueled for a jobsite other than their
                           assigned one that week
    Obra a mano            typed a location instead of picking one
    N cargas/7d            more than 4 fills in a week
    Domingo                Sunday
    Fuera de horario       before 5am or after 8pm
    N vehículos en un día  one person, 3+ different units in a day
  Thresholds live in the database. Tell me and I move them.
